# Problem Statement
Students struggle to plan their study schedules efficiently due to overlapping classes, deadlines, and exams. Manual planning causes time conflicts and stress.

# Project Objective
To develop a **Smart Studying Timetable Generator (SSTG)** that:
- Automatically generates personalized study timetables.
- Integrates exam dates, classes, and deadlines.
- Provides reminders and adapts to skipped sessions.
